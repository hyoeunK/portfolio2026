## Writing Notes

- Keep each section concrete and specific.
- Emphasize decisions, tradeoffs, and outcomes over decorative process.
- Be explicit about your contribution versus team contribution.
- Avoid vague statements unless you can support them with examples.
- If metrics cannot be shared, describe directional impact clearly.

## Asset Checklist

### Images

- Thumbnail / hero image for each project
- 3-6 supporting screens per project
- 1-2 diagrams, flows, or frameworks
- Anonymized versions of sensitive screens

### Video

- Only include if motion is essential to understanding the work
- Keep clips short, muted, and compressed
- Prefer `mp4` for web performance

### Resume

- Export a clean PDF
